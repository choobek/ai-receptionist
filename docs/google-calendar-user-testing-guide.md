# Google Calendar Connection Test Guide

Send this to the calendar owner.

## Steps

1. Open the link the clinic sent you.
2. On the page, click `Continue with Google`.
3. Sign in with the Google account that owns or manages the clinic calendar.
4. Approve calendar access when Google asks.
5. If you see a list of calendars, choose the calendar that should receive clinic bookings.
6. Wait for the final success message.

## What you should see

- a page branded for the clinic
- Google's normal sign-in and consent screens
- after login, either:
  - a calendar picker page, or
  - a success page saying the calendar is ready

## Success looks like this

- the final page says the Google Calendar connection is ready or saved
- if a calendar is shown, it matches the one you expect the clinic to use
- no error banner is shown

## If something fails

Send the clinic:

- a screenshot of the page
- the exact time it failed
- the Google account email you tried to use
- the exact error text if Google showed one

Do not keep retrying with different Google accounts unless the clinic asks you to.
